Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

TikTok Parcel Manager is a web application designed to help users organize account labels and parcel tracking information.

Information We Collect

Depending on how the service is used, we may process account labels, parcel tracking numbers, shipment statuses, and information users submit to the application.

How We Use Information

We use this information to organize parcels, display tracking information, and operate the service.

Data Storage and Security

Information is processed and stored on the application's server. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no system is completely secure.

Third-Party Services

The application may use hosting and shipping tracking services to provide its functionality. Additional integrations will be described when enabled.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as needed to operate the service. Users may contact the operator to request deletion of their information.

Contact

For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, contact the application operator through the contact information provided with the service.
